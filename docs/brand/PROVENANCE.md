# The heron engraving — provenance

**Subject:** Grey heron, *Ardea cinerea* ("Gråhägern").
**Source:** *Nordisk familjebok*, 2nd edition (Uggleupplagan), volume 12
(1910), page 83, wood engraving. Anonymous encyclopedia illustration.
**Scan:** Project Runeberg, page `nfbl/0058`, archival 1-bit TIFF
3680×5550 px — https://runeberg.org/nfbl/0058.html
**Also on Wikimedia Commons** (lower-resolution PNG cut from the same scan):
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gr%C3%A5h%C3%A4ger,_Nordisk_familjebok.png
**Licence:** public domain. Published 1910, more than 95 years ago; the
engraver is not named. Commons records it as `Public domain`.

**Processing (reproducible, no redrawing):** the bird was cropped from the
TIFF, the page's column rule and specks were removed, and the black ink was
turned into an alpha channel. Nothing was added or redrawn; the file
`heron-engraving-1080x1556.png` is the engraving as printed in 1910.

**Why a heron:** it stands still for hours and strikes once. ATRA's default
answer is `NO_ACTION`; the risk engine exists so that acting is rare and
deliberate. The bird is the behaviour.
